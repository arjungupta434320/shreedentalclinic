Clear Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our technology, pricing, and painless dental procedures.

Is root canal treatment (RCT) really painless at Shree Dental Care?
Yes, absolutely. We utilize computerized local anesthesia, precision electronic apex locators, and ultra-gentle nickel-titanium rotary files. Most of our root canals are completed in a single comfortable 45-minute sitting where patients feel zero pain or vibration.
How much do Swiss Dental Implants cost and how long do they last?
Our dental implants are sourced from world leaders like Straumann and Nobel Biocare, which fuse with your jawbone and last a lifetime with proper oral hygiene. Costs depend on bone condition and crown material, and we offer 0% interest EMI options with clear upfront quotes.
Are clear aligners effective for adults?
Yes! Over 60% of our clear aligner patients are adults. The aligners are virtually invisible, completely removable for meals and meetings, and fix crowding, gaps, and bite misalignments comfortably within 4 to 9 months.
What sterilization protocols do you practice?
We follow strict 7-tier hospital-grade sterilization using Class-B vacuum autoclaves, ultrasonic chemical disinfection, and UV storage. All instruments are packed in vacuum-sealed individual pouches opened exclusively in front of the patient.
Do you accept emergency visits or weekend walk-ins?
Yes! For severe toothache, trauma, or broken teeth, we have on-call emergency slots available 7 days a week. Call our direct hotline immediately at +91 81261 26513 for priority assistance.
